Plant App uses computer vision and AI to help plant owners care for their plants. Simply take a photo of your plant, and the app identifies it, provides care instructions, and sends reminders for watering, fertilizing, and other maintenance tasks.

Current development focus:
- Improving plant identification accuracy with rare species
- Building community features for plant advice
- Expanding care databases for region-specific guidance

Early beta users report 60% higher plant survival rates compared to before using the app.
