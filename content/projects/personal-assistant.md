An experimental project to build a privacy-first AI assistant that runs locally while optionally syncing to the cloud. The project taught valuable lessons about security architecture.

Why archived:
- Shifted focus to products with broader market fit
- Learned that consumer privacy products face significant adoption barriers
- These insights were applied to other projects

The codebase is open-source and serves as a reference for building privacy-conscious AI applications.
